# Lẩu Tokbokki ↔ 즉석떡볶이 (Jeukseok Tteokbokki) — mini game spec

Status: design approved 2026-11-04 (version 1), **implemented 2026-11-04**.

> Implementation notes:
> - **Reach is edge to edge.** A player can't get closer to the table's center than table radius + player radius, which is 1.04 units from the nearest plate center (4 players), more than `reach` 0.75. So reach is measured from the player's edge to the plate's edge (center distance ≤ reach + player radius + plate size / 2), and to the pot's edge for taps.
> - The 4 slots empty at the start stay empty; only a taken plate refills, so the table holds at most 8.
> - Taking a spoiled item out of your own pot can complete it, and that wins too.
> - A player can't re-pick an item they dropped on purpose for 1 s; a knocked-off item lands 0.55 units away from the bumper.
> - The countdown digits show in the timer pill and "Nấu lẩu thôi!" over the table; the timer reads m:ss in the round.
> - The art is held in `LauTokbokkiArt` on the game component, assigned by `ProjectSetup` (no hand-written USS paths), and the menu card uses `Hotpot/Full.png` as its icon.
> - Not done: audio (no `hotpot_*` clips). The ingredient names are in the Localization table but not shown anywhere yet.
> - Tuning to watch: a bot that always walks to the nearest needed plate finishes a perfect pot in about 4 s, so real rounds may rarely reach 60 s.
Read `CLAUDE.md` first; this spec follows its patterns (pure model + `PanelMiniGame` view, `*Settings` tuning, Localization table, `ProjectSetup` scene generation, PlayMode tests). Design page: `docs/LauTokbokki.html`.

**Depends on `docs/TachKeo.md`:** it uses the per-seat joysticks (`PlayerInputRouter.Stick` / `StickActive`) added there. Implement Tách Kẹo's router change first.

## Summary

Top-down, everyone at once, every player for themselves (2 or 4 players). A round table **spins slowly** in the middle of the floor with ingredient plates around its rim and the VN × KR flags in the center. Each player has a pen with their own hotpot near their seat and a random recipe of 5 ingredients. Players walk with their joystick, grab what they need from the spinning table, and carry it home to their pot. They can **steal** an ingredient from someone else's pot by tapping the joystick there, and **bump** each other to knock carried ingredients onto the floor.

- **Win:** the first player to complete a perfect hotpot (all 5 slots) wins and the round ends.
- **Time up (60 s):** the player with the most correct ingredients in their pot wins; if the top count is shared, it's a draw.

## Coordinates

**Arena units:** the floor is 16 × 9 units, origin bottom-left, y up (1 unit = 120 px at 1080 tall). The model works only in arena units. The view maps them to the panel with a uniform scale `stage.y / 9`, centered horizontally; on wider phones the floor texture just extends past the arena's sides (nothing playable there).

Layout (arena units):

| Thing | 4 players | 2 players |
|---|---|---|
| Table | center (8, 4.5), radius 2.35 | center (8, 4.5), radius 2.6 |
| Plates | 12 slots on a ring at 0.72 × table radius, plate size 0.72 | same, plate size 0.8 |
| Pens | P1 bottom-left x 2.9–6.4, y 0.25–2.75; P3 top-left x 2.9–6.4, y 6.25–8.75; P2 top-right x 9.6–13.1, y 6.25–8.75; P4 bottom-right x 9.6–13.1, y 0.25–2.75 | P1 left x 2.2–4.7, y 2.75–6.25; P2 right x 11.3–13.8, y 2.75–6.25 |
| Pot | pen center, radius 0.62 (solid) | same |
| Spawn | each player starts in their pen, beside the pot | same |

Pens follow the seats (`SeatLayout`), so a player's pen is right next to their joystick. Pens are floor zones with a colored fence drawn on three sides (open toward the table); anyone can walk in. The table and the pots are solid; arena edges are walls.

## Input

- **Move:** `PlayerInputRouter.Stick(id)` (screen space, unit disc). Rotate nothing: the floor is shared and seen from above, so stick right = walk right on screen for everyone.
- **Tap = action:** a short press on the joystick. In the view: a touch that lasts < `tapSeconds` (0.25 s) and whose stick never went above 0.3 is a tap → `_model.Action(player, time)`. The Editor keys Z / P / Q / M (existing seat press keys) also send a tap on release.
- Holding and dragging never counts as a tap.

## Model (`LauTokbokkiModel`)

Pure C#: explicit `double time`, fixed substeps (`step` = 1/120 s), seeded RNG. `SetStick(player, v, time)` advances to `time` with the previous stick, then stores the new one.

```csharp
public enum Ingredient { Tteok, Eomuk, Kimchi, Enoki, Greens, Egg }

public sealed class LauTokbokkiModel
{
    public LauTokbokkiModel(LauTokbokkiSettings s, int playerCount, int seed);
    public IReadOnlyList<Ingredient> RecipeOf(int player);   // 5 items, at most 2 of one type
    public IReadOnlyList<Ingredient> PotOf(int player);      // what's in the pot, in order added
    public int CorrectCount(int player);                     // pot items that match an open recipe slot
    public bool IsPerfect(int player);
    public Vector2 PositionOf(int player); public float FacingOf(int player);
    public Ingredient? CarriedBy(int player);
    public float TableAngle { get; }                         // radians, grows with time
    public IReadOnlyList<PlateState> Plates { get; }         // 12 slots: ingredient or empty, refill time
    public IReadOnlyList<FloorItem> FloorItems { get; }
    public int WinnerPlayer { get; }                          // -1 = none / draw
    public bool IsOver { get; }
    public void Start(double time);
    public void SetStick(int player, Vector2 stick, double time);
    public void Action(int player, double time);
    public void Advance(double time);
    public int[] Ranking();
    public event Action<LtEvent> Event; // PickedUp, Dropped, Stolen, Spoiled, KnockedOff, Refilled, Perfect, TimeUp
}
```

### Recipe

Each player gets their own random recipe: 5 items from the 6 types, at most 2 of one type (seeded). Recipes may overlap between players; that's what makes stealing worth it.

### Table

- Spins clockwise at `tableSpinDegPerSec` (10°/s, one turn in 36 s). Plates turn with it; the flags don't.
- 12 plate slots. At start, 8 are filled (random types, weighted toward what players need); 4 are empty.
- When a plate is taken, that slot **refills `refillSeconds` (1.5 s) later** with a new ingredient. Type choice: 70% a type at least one player still needs (counting what's in pots and hands), 30% random. Never more than 12 on the table.

### Moving and bumping

- Players are circles (radius 0.38). Velocity eases toward `stick * walkSpeed` (3.6 units/s) with acceleration 18 units/s². Facing follows the velocity.
- Collisions: player–player (equal mass, they push apart), player–table, player–pot, player–arena edge.
- **Bump:** if two players touch and their closing speed is above `bumpSpeed` (2.8 units/s ≈ 80% of full speed), each player who is carrying something and was hit *by the faster player* drops it onto the floor beside them (`KnockedOff`). The faster player keeps theirs. A knocked player is stunned for `bumpStun` (0.4 s): no movement, can't pick up.

### Picking up (automatic)

When empty-handed:
- **Table:** a plate whose center is within `reach` (0.75) of the player's center, and whose ingredient the player's recipe still needs (not already in their pot or hand), is taken. The plate slot empties and starts its refill timer.
- **Floor:** same rule for floor items. Floor items vanish after `floorSeconds` (8 s); their type goes back into the spawn pool.

### Putting in a pot

- **Own pot, automatic:** entering your own pen while carrying an ingredient your recipe still needs drops it into your pot.
- **Any pot, by tap:** tapping within `reach` of a pot (yours or a rival's) while carrying puts the ingredient in that pot.
  - If that pot's recipe still needs it, it fills a slot (counts for that player).
  - If not, it's a **spoiled** item (`Spoiled`): it sits in the pot, does **not** count, and the pot can't be perfect while it's there. The pot owner taps at their own pot while empty-handed to take the spoiled item out into their hand (then drop it on the floor with a tap away from any pot, or put it in someone else's pot).
- **Tap with nothing nearby:** drops the carried ingredient onto the floor.

### Stealing

Tap within `reach` of a **rival's** pot while empty-handed: take the **most recently added** ingredient out of it into your hand (`Stolen`). Correct items come out first; spoiled items are never stolen (only the owner removes those). Then you can carry it to your own pot (fills a slot if you need it) or put it in any other pot.
Each pot has a `stealCooldown` (1.5 s) after being stolen from, so a pot can't be emptied in a second.

### Perfect and end

- A pot is **perfect** when all 5 recipe slots are filled and it contains no spoiled item. First perfect pot → that player wins, `Perfect` event, round ends immediately.
- At `roundSeconds` (60 s) with no perfect pot: compare `CorrectCount`. The single highest wins. If two or more share the highest count → draw (`WinnerPlayer = -1`).

### Ranking (for 3/2/1/0 points)

- Perfect pot: that player rank 1, everyone else ranked by `CorrectCount` (ties share).
- Time up: everyone by `CorrectCount`, ties share; if the top is shared they share rank 1 and `HasWinner` is false.

- **Floor:** warm tiled restaurant floor (USS color + subtle grid is fine for v1).
- **Table:** `Table.png` rotated by `TableAngle`. Plates drawn on top at their slot positions (they turn with the table). The flags sprite on the table's center plate, not rotated. Empty slots show nothing; a refilling plate pops in with a small scale bounce.
- **Pens:** a colored fence (player color) on three sides, open toward the table; floor tinted with the player color at 15%.
- **Pot:** `Empty.png` at the pen's pot spot; the ingredients inside are shown as small plate icons arranged in a ring inside the pot (spoiled ones with a grey tint and a small ✕). Perfect → swap to `Full.png`, steam, gold ring.
- **Recipe card:** next to each pot, rotated with the seat (top players upside down): 5 slots, each showing the needed ingredient; filled slots get a check in the player color.
- **Players:** `VN_5` (P1, P3) / `KR_4` (P2, P4), ~0.9 units wide, rotated to face their movement, with a ring in the player color underneath. A carried ingredient floats above the head (plate at 55%). Stun: small stars for `bumpStun`.
- **Joysticks:** same visuals as Tách Kẹo (base ring + knob at the seat anchor, rotated with the seat). The knob flashes on a tap.
- **Counters:** each recipe card shows `n/5`. Timer pill at the top center (or bottom center for 2 players, whichever is free) counting down from 60.
- **Feedback text** near the pot, facing its owner: "Bị trộm!" when stolen from, "Hỏng!" when spoiled, "+1" when a slot fills.

## Tests (`LauTokbokkiModelTests.cs`)

Model-only, explicit times, fixed seed:
- Recipes: 5 items, ≤ 2 of a type, different seeds → different recipes; each player's recipe is independent.
- Table: angle = `tableSpinDegPerSec × t`; 8 plates at start; a taken plate refills after exactly `refillSeconds`; never more than 12.
- Pickup: only needed types; nothing while carrying; nothing while stunned; a plate out of `reach` isn't taken; a plate turns into reach as the table spins and is taken.
- Own pen: entering with a needed item fills a slot; entering with an unneeded item does nothing.
- Tap at a rival pot with a needed-by-them item fills their slot; with an unneeded item → spoiled, doesn't count, blocks perfect; owner tap removes it into their hand.
- Steal: takes the most recently added correct item; respects `stealCooldown`; can't steal while carrying; can't steal spoiled items; can't steal from own pot.
- Bump: closing speed above `bumpSpeed` knocks the slower player's item to the floor and stuns them; below it, nothing drops; floor items expire after `floorSeconds`.
- Collisions keep players out of the table, pots and outside the arena.
- End: first perfect pot ends the round with that winner; at 60 s the highest `CorrectCount` wins; shared highest → draw, `HasWinner` false; ranking ties share.
- Frame-rate independence: the same scripted sticks at 30 fps and 144 fps give the same positions (± 1e-4) and events.

Play-mode: a `MenuFlowTests` case that taps the card and reaches the scene; a check that every asset in the table above loads (non-null `Texture2D`/`Sprite`) in the scene.
