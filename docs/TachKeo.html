Tách Kẹo 뽑기 · Ppopgi / Dalgona

Up to four players cut honeycomb candy at the same time, each in their own part of the phone with their own random shape. You move a needle with your own joystick and follow the stamped line. Go off the line or push too hard and the candy cracks, so you start a new one. Only the finishing order counts: 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th. The round lasts 20 seconds.

A round

1 · Show the shape

Everyone gets a shape

During a 3 s countdown, each candy is stamped with a random shape and its name. No two players get the same one.

2 · Cut

Trace the line

Drag your joystick to move the needle. The line darkens where you have carved it, and the ring around your tin shows how much is done.

3 · Crack

Start again

If the crack meter fills, the candy crumbles. After a 1 s pause you get a fresh candy with 0% progress. Your clock keeps running.

4 · Pop

Shape comes out

Carve the whole outline and the shape pops out of the candy. Your time is locked in and your corner celebrates.

The joystick

Each seat button becomes a joystick in the same place: the corner with 4 players, the middle of the short edge with 2. It's a floating stick: put your thumb down anywhere in your corner and drag. The needle moves in the direction you drag, faster the further you drag. Letting go stops the needle where it is, without lifting it off the candy.

  • Top speed: about 1.1 candy-widths… precisely 260 px/s at full tilt on the 4-player candy (460 px across). The 2-player candy is bigger (680 px), and the needle scales with it so both feel the same.
  • The needle stays inside the candy, and the line pulls it in slightly (a light magnet within 10 px) so the controls don't feel slippery.
  • For Editor testing: P1 uses WASD, P2 the arrow keys, P3 IJKL and P4 the numpad 8/4/5/6.

Why candy cracks

One crack meter per player fills for two reasons and slowly heals when you cut carefully. Cracks spread out from the needle as it fills, so you can see the danger before the candy breaks.

With these numbers, a careful cut takes about 8–12 seconds, so a player can afford one crack and still finish within 20 s. If nobody finishes, that's fine: the ranking still sorts everyone by progress.

Winning

Fastest clean cut

Finished players rank by their time. The fastest wins.

Time's up at 20 s

Players who didn't finish rank below every finisher, by how much of their current candy is carved.

Ties

Equal times or equal progress share the same rank. A candy that is broken when time runs out counts as 0%. If nobody carved anything, nobody wins.

Tournament points use the usual 3/2/1/0. This is the first Madang game where everyone plays for themselves; it doesn't use teams.

Shapes

Each round the four shapes are shuffled, and every player gets a different one. All four outlines are scaled to the same length, so no shape is a shortcut. The umbrella's corners and hook still make it the hardest one.

Look and sound

  • A street-vendor tray seen from above: a round tin for each player, a honeycomb candy and a thin needle.
  • The carved line turns dark brown, and cracks are thin dark lines spreading from the needle.
  • Breaking: the candy crumbles into a few pieces with a crunch, then a fresh candy slides in.
  • Pop: the shape lifts out with a little bounce and a chime.
  • Sounds: a soft scratch while carving, faster ticking clicks as the crack meter fills, a crunch when it breaks and a chime when the shape pops.

Fun facts

  • Ppopgi is melted sugar with a pinch of baking soda, pressed flat and stamped with a shape. It was a popular Korean street snack in the 1970s and 80s.
  • The vendor's deal: cut the shape out without breaking it and you get another candy for free.
  • “Dalgona” comes from 달다, “sweet”. In Vietnam the candy is called kẹo đường, sugar candy, and the challenge is called tách kẹo, splitting the candy.

Keeping it Madang: no guards, masks, tracksuits or doll, and no “eliminated” moments. Breaking only means you get a new candy, and the street-stall setting keeps it reading as a snack game.

Decisions

  • 2 players: each player gets half the screen and a bigger candy.
  • Joysticks: the touch input script reports each player's drag direction. This game uses a joystick per seat instead of one button.
  • Cracking: a fresh candy at 0% after 1 s, and your clock keeps running.
  • Winning: only the order counts: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th. It's fine if nobody finishes.
  • Shapes: umbrella, square, oval and star, shuffled so nobody shares a shape.
