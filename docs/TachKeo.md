# Tách Kẹo ↔ Ppopgi / Dalgona (뽑기 / 달고나) — mini game spec

Status: design approved 2026-11-04 (version 1), **implemented 2026-11-04**.

> Implementation notes: carving and cracking happen only while the stick is active, so a candy nobody touches stays at 0% (otherwise the needle would carve its start segments on its own and nobody could ever have 0 progress). With 2 players on a 16:9 screen, a 340 px candy plus the 120 px shift makes the two tins overlap, so the tins shrink to fit between joystick and midline (`TachKeoGame.FitTin`). The countdown shows in the timer pill (centered with 4 players), and the shape name and "Chuẩn bị tách kẹo!" show on each candy. All three fun facts are used, one per round. The tournament subtitle now says 5 games. Not done: audio (no `tachkeo_*` clips) and the art PNGs (everything is drawn in code). Known quirk: the umbrella's shaft is 0.073 wide after scaling, so a needle down its middle carves both edges at once.
Read `CLAUDE.md` first; this spec follows its patterns (pure model + `PanelMiniGame` view, `*Settings` tuning, Localization table, `ProjectSetup` scene generation, PlayMode tests). Design page: `docs/TachKeo.html`.

## Summary

Everyone plays at once, every player for themselves (no teams). Each player has a honeycomb candy in a round tin in their part of the screen, stamped with a shape. They move a needle with **their own joystick** and trace the stamped outline. Going off the line or pushing the stick too hard fills a crack meter; when it's full the candy breaks and the player starts again on a fresh candy (their clock keeps running). Carve the whole outline and the shape pops out. **Ranking is all that matters:** finishers by time, then everyone else by how much of their current candy is carved. Round: 20 s.

- 4 players: one quarter of the screen each.
- 2 players: one half of the screen each (bigger candy).
- **Every player gets a different shape**, picked at random each round from: umbrella, square, oval, star.

Visual direction: a street-vendor candy tray seen from above. No guards, masks, tracksuits, doll or "eliminated" moments; breaking only means a new candy.

## Input: joysticks (`PlayerInputRouter` change)

The router keeps emitting `PlayerInput(id, pressed)` exactly as now. Add a polled stick per player:

- `public Vector2 Stick(int playerId)`: unit-disc vector (magnitude 0..1).
  - Touch: the finger that owns the seat (sticky to the zone it started in, as now). Vector = (current position − touch-start position) / `StickRadius`, clamped to length 1. `StickRadius` = 9% of screen height in pixels. Zero when no finger is down.
  - Editor keyboard: P1 `W A S D`, P2 arrow keys, P3 `I J K L`, P4 numpad `8 4 5 6`. Diagonals normalized to length 1.
  - Editor mouse: a drag that starts in a seat's zone drives that seat's stick, same formula.
- `public bool StickActive(int playerId)`: a finger (or key/mouse) is driving it.
- Screen y is up in the router's normalized space; return the vector in **screen space** (x right, y up). The game rotates it into each player's candy space.
- Statics reset with `[RuntimeInitializeOnLoadMethod(SubsystemRegistration)]` (domain reload is off).
- Mini games still never read touches directly.

Tests (`PlayerInputRouterTests`, with `InputTestFixture`):
- Begin a touch in P1's zone and move it right by half `StickRadius` → `Stick(0) ≈ (0.5, 0)`; by 2 × `StickRadius` → length 1.
- The stick stays with P1 when the finger moves into another zone; zero after lift.
- Two fingers in two zones drive two sticks independently.
- Keyboard: `D` → `Stick(0) = (1, 0)`; `W`+`D` → length 1.
- Existing press tests still pass (`PlayerInput` events unchanged).

## Coordinates

**Candy units:** candy radius = 1, center (0, 0), +y = away from the player (toward the top of the candy as the player sees it). All model distances and speeds are in candy units, so 2- and 4-player layouts play the same.

The view maps candy units to the screen with the candy's center, its radius in panel px and the seat's rotation (`SeatLayout` rotation: 0 / 180 for 4 players, −90 / 90 for 2 players). The stick vector from the router is rotated by the inverse seat rotation before being passed to the model, so "push the stick away from you" always moves the needle up the candy for that player.

## Shapes (`TachKeoShapes`)

`enum TachKeoShape { Umbrella, Square, Oval, Star }`.
`static Vector2[] Outline(TachKeoShape shape, int samples)`: a closed polyline, evenly resampled by arc length to `samples` points, starting at the shape's topmost point and going clockwise.

All outlines are scaled so **their perimeter is the same** (`outlinePerimeter`, default 3.4 candy units), so finishing times are comparable across shapes. Every point must stay within radius 0.85. Shape definitions before scaling:

- **Square:** side 1, corners exactly square.
- **Oval:** ellipse, width 1.3, height 0.9.
- **Star:** 5 points, outer radius 1, inner radius 0.42, a point at the top.
- **Umbrella:** a dome (half ellipse, width 1.3, height 0.65) with a scalloped bottom edge (3 shallow arcs), a short straight shaft down from the middle, ending in a J-hook. Trace it as one closed outline around the whole silhouette (shaft and hook have width 0.12).

Unit test: each outline's perimeter = `outlinePerimeter` ± 1%, all points within 0.85, first point is the topmost, no duplicate consecutive points.

**Assignment:** at `Setup`, shuffle the four shapes with the round's seed and give player `id` the shape at index `id`. No two players share a shape. 2 players get 2 different shapes.

## Model (`TachKeoModel`)

```csharp
public enum CutState { Waiting, Cutting, Broken, Done }

public sealed class TachKeoModel
{
    public TachKeoModel(TachKeoSettings settings, int playerCount, int seed);
    public TachKeoShape ShapeOf(int player);
    public IReadOnlyList<Vector2> OutlineOf(int player);     // candy units
    public CutState StateOf(int player);
    public Vector2 NeedleOf(int player);                      // candy units
    public float CrackOf(int player);                         // 0..1
    public float ProgressOf(int player);                      // 0..1 of the current candy
    public bool IsCarved(int player, int segment);            // for drawing the carved line
    public int SegmentCount { get; }
    public double FinishTimeOf(int player);                   // seconds since Start, NaN if not done
    public int BreaksOf(int player);
    public bool IsOver { get; }
    public void Start(double time);                            // Waiting -> Cutting for everyone
    public void SetStick(int player, Vector2 stick, double time); // candy-space stick, 0..1
    public void Advance(double time);                          // fixed substeps up to time
    public int[] Ranking();                                    // 1 = best, ties share
    public event Action<TachKeoEvent> Event;                   // Cracking(player, level), Broke, Restarted, Finished, TimeUp
}
```

Pure C#: explicit `double time` everywhere, no `Time`, no MonoBehaviour. `SetStick` first advances to `time` with the previous stick, then stores the new one (so results don't depend on frame rate). Fixed substep `step` (1/240 s).

### Per substep, for each player in `Cutting`

1. **Move:** `velocity = stick * needleSpeed`. Within `magnetRadius` of the outline, pull the needle toward the nearest outline point by `magnetStrength * dt` (never past it). Clamp the needle inside radius 0.95.
2. **Carve:** every segment whose midpoint is within `carveTolerance` of the needle is carved. A single uncarved segment between two carved neighbours is also carved (fills pixel gaps).
3. **Crack** (only while the stick is active, `|stick| > 0.05`):
   - `d` = distance from the needle to the outline. If `d > carveTolerance`: `crack += offLineRate * (1 + (d - carveTolerance) / carveTolerance) * dt`.
   - If `|stick| > pushThreshold`: `crack += pushRate * (|stick| - pushThreshold) / (1 - pushThreshold) * dt`.
   - If on the line and `|stick| ≤ pushThreshold`: `crack -= healRate * dt` (not below 0).
   - Emit `Cracking(player, level)` when crack crosses 0.5 and 0.8 (for sound/visual tiers).
4. **Break:** `crack ≥ 1` → `Broken`, `BreaksOf++`, emit `Broke`. After `restartDelay`: fresh candy, same shape, crack 0, all segments uncarved, needle at the outline's first point → `Cutting`, emit `Restarted`. The round clock keeps running.
5. **Done:** all segments carved → `Done`, record `FinishTimeOf`, emit `Finished`. Input for that player is ignored afterwards.

The round ends (`IsOver`, emit `TimeUp` or after the last `Finished`) when every player is `Done` or `roundSeconds` have passed since `Start`.

### Ranking

1. `Done` players first, ordered by `FinishTimeOf` (lower is better).
2. Then everyone else, by `ProgressOf` of their current candy (higher is better). A player in `Broken` at time-up counts as 0.
3. Equal time or equal progress share a rank.
4. `HasWinner` is false only if nobody finished and everyone's progress is 0.

`GetRanking()` returns this; `GameManager` turns it into 3/2/1/0 points as for other games. Finishing order is what counts; it doesn't matter if nobody finishes.

## Settings (`[Serializable] TachKeoSettings` on `TachKeoGame`)

| Field | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `roundSeconds` | 20 | version 1 |
| `outlinePerimeter` | 3.4 | candy units, same for every shape |
| `segments` | 160 | carve resolution |
| `needleSpeed` | 1.13 | candy units/s at full stick (= 260 px/s on a 460 px candy) |
| `carveTolerance` | 0.06 | how close counts as "on the line" |
| `magnetRadius` / `magnetStrength` | 0.045 / 0.4 | gentle pull onto the line |
| `pushThreshold` | 0.7 | stick tilt above this stresses the candy |
| `offLineRate` | 1.2 | crack per second just off the line |
| `pushRate` | 0.6 | crack per second at full tilt |
| `healRate` | 0.15 | crack recovery per second when careful |
| `restartDelay` | 1.0 | seconds between breaking and the new candy |
| `step` | 1/240 | fixed substep |

Expected feel: a careful full trace takes about 3.4 / (1.13 × 0.7) ≈ 4.3 s of pure movement, about 8–12 s in practice with corners and corrections, so one crack still leaves time to finish.

## View (`TachKeoGame : PanelMiniGame`)

- Implements `Bind(root)`, `FunFacts`, `Setup(playerCount)`, `OnPlayerInput` (ignored except as "stick active" if useful), `GetRanking()`; calls `EndRound(title)`. HUD must contain `seatLayer`, `timer`, `centerMessage`, `togglePlayers`, `ResultsOverlay.uxml`, `PauseOverlay.uxml`.
- Every frame (not while paused): for each player, rotate `PlayerInputRouter.Stick(id)` into candy space and call `_model.SetStick(id, v, Now)`; then `_model.Advance(Now)`. Use `Now` (PausableClock), never `Time.time`.
- Countdown 3 s ("Chuẩn bị tách kẹo!"), during which each candy shows its shape and its name.

### Layout (sizes from stage height so phones of any width work)

- **4 players:** one tin per quadrant, rotated with the seat (top two upside down). Candy radius `0.213 * stage.y` (230 px at 1080). Candy center = quadrant center moved 80 px toward the screen's vertical midline (away from the corner joystick). Tin radius = candy × 1.12.
- **2 players:** one tin per half, rotated −90 / 90 with the seat. Candy radius `0.315 * stage.y` (340 px). Candy center = half's center moved 120 px toward the screen's midline. Timer pill at the top center.
- Verify at 16:9 and a tall phone that a tin never overlaps its joystick or another tin.

### Joysticks (replace the seat discs in this scene)

- The joystick sits where the seat disc is (`SeatLayout` anchor): a base ring (170 px, player color) and a knob (70 px) that moves up to 50 px in the stick's direction, so players see their input. Rotated with the seat.
- Input is floating (from where the thumb lands in the player's zone), but the visual stays at the seat, so the thumb naturally goes there and doesn't cover the candy.
- Under the joystick: player name and live status ("45%", "Vỡ rồi!", "9.4 s").

### Candy drawing

- Tin: grey metal disc with a rim. Candy: honey color with a darker edge and a subtle bubbly texture (sprite or `generateVisualContent` noise).
- Outline: drawn with `Painter2D` in `generateVisualContent` as a dashed stamped line; carved segments drawn on top as a thick dark groove.
- Needle: thin grey pin, tip on the needle position, pointing away from the player.
- Progress ring around the tin (player color), length = `ProgressOf`.
- Cracks: thin dark lines spreading from the needle; 0 lines below 0.5 crack, 2 lines at 0.5, 4 longer lines at 0.8, with a small shake of the candy at ≥ 0.8.
- Break: candy splits into 4–5 pieces that slide apart and fade (0.6 s), then a fresh candy slides in from the player's side.
- Done: the shape lifts (scale 1.08, shadow) with a little bounce, the progress ring turns gold, and the joystick shows the time.

### Center and results

- Timer pill: seconds left (20 → 0).
- When everyone is done or time is up → `EndRound`, title "P2 thắng!" (via `Localization.Won`), results card as other games.

## Tests (`TachKeoModelTests.cs`)

Model-only, explicit times, fixed seed:
- Shapes: perimeter equal ± 1%; inside radius 0.85; start at the topmost point.
- Assignment: with 4 players all four shapes are used once; with 2 players two different shapes; same seed → same assignment.
- A scripted perfect trace (stick follows the outline direction at `pushThreshold` speed) finishes with 0 breaks; `FinishTimeOf` ≈ perimeter / (needleSpeed × pushThreshold) ± 10%.
- Holding the stick sideways off the line breaks the candy in under 1 s; after `restartDelay` the player is `Cutting` again with progress 0 and needle at the start point; the round clock did not reset.
- Full tilt along the line cracks slowly (breaks between 1.5 s and 3 s); 0.6 tilt along the line never breaks.
- Healing: crack drops by `healRate` per second when careful, never below 0.
- No crack while the stick is released, even off the line.
- Single-gap fill: carving segments 1 and 3 also carves 2.
- Frame-rate independence: the same scripted input at 30 fps and 144 fps gives the same progress and crack (± 1e-4).
- Ranking: finishers by time; then by progress; Broken at time-up counts as 0; ties share; nobody progressed → `HasWinner` false.
- Round ends at `roundSeconds`, or early once everyone is `Done`.

Play-mode: a `MenuFlowTests` case that taps the Tách Kẹo card and reaches the scene (unload in `[UnityTearDown]`).
