Bắn Bi 구슬치기 · Guseul-chigi

Top-down team marbles. Red is Vietnam, blue is Korea. One hand below the square slides along the edge. Stop it, pull back and flick to knock the other team's marbles out of the zone.

1v1 or 2v23 marbles per teamSlide · hold · release~2–3 min

The hand art in the game is VN_4 on red's turns and KR_3 on blue's turns.

How a round goes

1 · Dice

Roll for order

Everyone taps to stop their die. The highest goes first, then turns alternate between the teams.

2 · Setup

3 + 3 marbles

Three red and three blue marbles are spread randomly inside the square.

3 · Turn

Your team's hand

The Vietnamese hand with a red marble on red's turn, the Korean hand with a blue marble on blue's turn. It's always in the same place, and your seat glows.

4 · Resolve

Marbles stop

Marbles outside the square are removed. Your shot marble stays where it stopped.

One button: slide, pull, flick

The hand slides back and forth along the bottom edge. Press to stop it where you want, hold to pull back, and release to shoot straight up. The dashed line grows with power to show about where the marble will stop. Dots past the top edge fade out, which means too much power.

After the marbles stop

A marble is out when its center is outside the square.

What happenedResult
Your shot stops inside the squareIt stays and becomes one of your team's marbles.
Your shot falls short or goes out the far sideThat marble is lost. “Hụt!”
You knock a rival marble outIt's removed, and you get a bonus shot, up to 2 in a row.
You knock your own team's marble outIt's removed. No bonus.

Winning

Empty square

After any shot, a team with no marbles left in the square loses right away. If both teams are empty, the round is a draw.

Shot limit

Each team gets 8 regular shots; in 2v2 that's 4 each. After that, the team with more marbles in the square wins.

Tiebreak

The team with the marble closest to the center wins. If that is still tied, the round is a draw.

Easy to see

  • The square is light sand with a thick dark chalk border.
  • The marbles are bright red and strong blue, with a white rim and a thin dark outline.
  • The hand gets a soft shadow behind it, and a team-colored “P1” chip under it.
  • Counters at the top show how many marbles each team has in the square, like VN ● 3 and KR ● 3.

What changed from v1

  • View: top-down square instead of a side-on ring with characters
  • Marbles: 3 per team instead of neutrals plus one per player
  • Aim: slide along the edge instead of a swinging arrow
  • Shots: marbles stay in the zone, so it fills up
  • Winning: count marbles instead of points and eliminations
