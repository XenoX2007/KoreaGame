Bắn Bi 구슬치기 · Guseul-chigi

Turn-based ring marbles. Flick your marble with one button, knock the neutral marbles out for points, and knock the other team's marbles out to eliminate them.

1v1 or 2v2VN vs KR teamsHold & release~2–3 min

How a round goes

1 · Dice

Roll for order

Each seat's die spins. Tap to stop it, or it stops by itself after 3 s. The highest roll goes first, then turns alternate between teams. Players who tie roll again.

2 · Setup

Ring is ready

Five glass marbles sit in the middle. Each shooter marble starts on the ring edge nearest its seat.

3 · Turn

Your hand appears

A Vietnamese sleeve for the left team, a hanbok sleeve for the right. The other seats dim. You have 10 s to shoot.

4 · Resolve

Marbles stop

Check what left the ring, add points, give bonus shots, then pass the turn to the next player.

One button, three moments

The aim arrow sweeps ±45° toward the ring center, or toward the nearest rival if your marble is already inside. Press to lock the aim, hold to pull back, and release to flick. Power rises for 1.2 s and then falls again, so holding too long gives a weak shot.

What leaving the ring means

A marble is out once its center crosses the ring line. A marble that stays inside takes its next shot from where it stopped.

Marble knocked outPointsWhat happens
Neutral glass marble+1Removed. The shooter gets a bonus shot, up to 2 in a row.
A rival's marble+2That player is eliminated for the rest of the round. Bonus shot.
Your own or your teammate's0Not eliminated. It shoots again from its seat edge next turn.

Winning

Clear the rivals

If every rival marble has been knocked out, your team wins right away.

Turn limit

Otherwise the round ends after 6 turns per player. The team with more points wins. Bonus shots don't count toward the limit.

Tiebreak

The team with the marble closest to the center wins. If that is still tied, the round is a draw.

Art

  • Vietnamese and Korean hands, each with a pulled-back pose and a flick pose
  • Marbles in the four seat colors, a clear glass marble, ground shadows
  • A scratched dirt ring, drawn side-on to fit the 60% horizon
  • Die faces 1–6, the aim arrow and the power arc

Sound

  • Marble clack, louder on harder hits
  • Rolling loop that follows speed
  • A drop when a marble leaves the ring
  • Dice rattle and stop
