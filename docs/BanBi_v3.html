Version 3

Bắn Bi 구슬치기 · Guseul-chigi

Top-down team marbles in a big round zone. Red is Vietnam, blue is Korea. The circle fills most of the screen, with the seat buttons right next to its edge. One hand at the bottom slides back and forth, and everyone uses the same shoot button right beside it on their turn. Stop the hand, pull back and flick to knock the other team's marbles out of the circle.

1v1 or 2v23 marbles per teamOne shared shoot button~2–3 min

Layout with 4 players. The VN_4 arm appears on red's turns and the KR_3 arm on blue's turns, reaching in from the bottom of the screen. The round red button beside it is the shared shoot button.

How a round goes

1 · Dice

Roll for order

Everyone taps to stop their die. The highest goes first, then turns alternate between the teams.

2 · Setup

3 + 3 marbles

Three red and three blue marbles are spread randomly near the middle of the circle.

3 · Turn

Your team's hand

The Vietnamese hand with a red marble on red's turn, the Korean hand with a blue marble on blue's turn. It's always in the same place, and your seat glows.

4 · Resolve

Marbles stop

Marbles outside the circle are removed. Your shot marble stays where it stopped.

One shared button: slide, pull, flick

It's turn-based, so on your turn you use the big shoot button right of the hand, not your own seat button. Your eyes stay on the hand, the aim line and the button together. Seat buttons are only for stopping your die at the start, and the button's color follows the team whose turn it is. The hand slides back and forth under the circle. Press to stop it where you want, hold to pull back, and release to shoot straight up. The dashed line grows with power to show about where the marble will stop. Dots outside the circle fade out, which means too much power.

After the marbles stop

A marble is out when its center is outside the circle.

What happenedResult
Your shot stops inside the circleIt stays and becomes one of your team's marbles.
Your shot falls short or goes out the far sideThat marble is lost. “Hụt!”
You knock a rival marble outIt's removed, and you get a bonus shot, up to 2 in a row.
You knock your own team's marble outIt's removed. No bonus.

Winning

Empty circle

After any shot, a team with no marbles left in the circle loses right away. If both teams are empty, the round is a draw.

Shot limit

Each team gets 8 regular shots; in 2v2 that's 4 each. After that, the team with more marbles in the circle wins.

Tiebreak

The team with the marble closest to the center wins. If that is still tied, the round is a draw.

Bigger and easier to see

  • The circle is 940 px across on a 1080 px tall screen, about 87% of the height.
  • Marbles are about 80 px across, nearly twice as big on screen as in v2.
  • The hand, the marble it holds and the aim dots are all 40% bigger.
  • Seat buttons move in to sit just outside the circle in this game only. Touch areas don't change.
  • Team counters and the timer sit in the side strips, beside the circle.

What changed from v2

  • Zone: a circle instead of a square
  • Size: the zone and marbles are much bigger, and sized from the screen height so they fit any phone
  • Seats: pulled in right next to the circle
  • HUD: counters, timer and hint moved to the side strips
  • Controls: one shared shoot button beside the hand for every turn; seat buttons only stop the dice
  • Rules: unchanged
