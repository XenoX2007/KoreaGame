Version 1 · approved Madang · tournament setup

Cup Selection

Madang now has six games, so Giải đấu opens a cup screen before anything starts. Pick the Mini Cup and choose 3 games yourself or let the dice pick them. Or pick the Grand Cup and play all 6, with the dice shuffling the order.

Two cups

Cúp Nhỏ · Mini Cup

3 games, about 5 minutes.

  • Tap a game to add it. It gets the next number: 1, 2, 3.
  • Tap a numbered game to remove it. The numbers after it move down.
  • A 4th tap just shakes the tile and shows “Đã chọn đủ 3 trò”.
  • The dice picks 3 random games and replaces your picks.

Cúp Lớn · Grand Cup

All 6 games, about 10 minutes.

  • Every game is already numbered, in story order: Người Gỗ, Kéo Co, Đá Cầu, Bắn Bi, Tách Kẹo, Lẩu Tokbokki.
  • Games can't be removed.
  • The dice shuffles the order.

The dice roll

The result is decided first, then shown in about 1.4 s. Tap anywhere to skip to the end, and the result stays the same.

1 · SpinThe die on the button spins and changes faces.
2 · HopA gold ring hops between the tiles, fast at first, then slowing.
3 · LandIt lands on the 1st game and the “1” badge pops in. Then it hops on to the 2nd and 3rd.
4 · ReadyThe order appears above Bắt đầu, and the button lights up.

How it fits in

Menu→Giải đấu→Cup screen→Game 1→Results “Trò 1/3”→…→Podium “Cúp Nhỏ”
  • The cup screen is a full-screen panel in the Menu scene, like the online and language panels, so it opens instantly and keeps the 2 or 4 player choice from the menu.
  • Scoring doesn't change: 3/2/1/0 points per game, then the podium.
  • Results screens show which game you're on, like “Trò 2/3”. The podium shows the cup's name and the small icons of the games that were played.
  • The screen remembers your last cup and your last 3 picks for next time.
  • A game that isn't in the build is greyed out, and the dice never picks it.

Code changes, in short

  • CupPicker: a new list of all 6 games with their icons and names, plus the dice logic (pick 3 or shuffle 6). It's plain code, so it's easy to test.
  • GameManager.StartTournament takes the cup type and the chosen game list instead of a fixed list. It shows the stage number (“Trò x/y”).
  • MainMenu: the Giải đấu button opens the cup panel. CupPanel runs the panel, like OnlinePanel.
  • Tests: the dice is random but fair, the tile picking works, and a Mini Cup plays exactly the 3 chosen games and then the podium.
